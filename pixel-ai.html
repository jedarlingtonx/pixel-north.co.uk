What Pixel AI does

  • Answers from your own pagesIt reads your live site, so what it says matches what you've published, and it keeps up when you change a page.
  • Ready when you're notA question at ten at night gets a helpful answer, not silence, so fewer visitors give up and leave.
  • Stays on topicIt only talks about your business. If the answer isn't there, it says it isn't sure and points people to you.
  • Hands over to youQuotes, bookings and anything personal go to your contact form or email. It never promises anything on your behalf.

How it works on your site

  1. We read your site. Your pages, services, FAQs and policies become the information Pixel answers from.
  2. We write its rules. Tone of voice, the links it may offer and the things it must never do, such as guessing prices you don't publish.
  3. You test it first. We set it up on a private preview, and you put your trickiest customer questions to it before anything goes live.
  4. It goes live across your site. A small button, styled to match your brand, opens the chat. We keep it healthy as your site and the technology change.

Good to know

  • HostingPixel AI runs on Cloudflare, so your site needs to be hosted there too. If it isn't yet, we can move it.
  • PrivacyWhat a visitor types is sent to Cloudflare's AI service to write a reply. We don't keep a record of it, the chat doesn't use cookies, and it asks visitors not to share personal details. We add the matching wording to your privacy notice.
  • AccuracyIt's an AI, so it can occasionally get something wrong. It's told to answer only from your information, to say when it isn't sure, and the chat reminds visitors of that.
  • Looking after itAI models are retired and replaced from time to time. Keeping Pixel AI working and up to date is part of looking after it.

Questions about Pixel AI

Will it replace talking to my customers?

No. It handles the everyday questions so people get a quick answer, and it passes anything that needs a human, such as a quote or a booking, to you.

Can it take bookings or payments?

Not at the moment. It answers questions and points people to your contact form or booking tool.

What if it gets an answer wrong?

We test it with you before it goes live, and you can ask us to change what it knows or how it answers at any time. The chat also tells visitors that AI answers can be wrong.

What does it cost?

Every business needs something different. Tell us what you have in mind and a written quotation follows a short initial consultation.

Can I switch it off?

Yes. It can be removed whenever you like, and from just specific pages too.

What happens to what visitors type?

It's sent to Cloudflare's AI service to write a reply. We don't keep a record, and the chat doesn't use cookies. You can read how this site handles it in the privacy notice.
