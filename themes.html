Seasonal themes for your website.

A small, tasteful touch that shows your business is awake and paying attention. Here are the themes we've designed for the Pixel North home page, and how one could work for yours.

Last updated 7 November 2026

What is a seasonal theme?

It's a light layer of decoration on top of your normal design: a banner, a few illustrations and maybe a gentle animation. Your content, layout and branding stay exactly as they are, so nothing gets harder to read or find.

Themes are discreet by design, and anyone who prefers less movement doesn't see the animation. It's a good way to mark Christmas, Easter, Remembrance, a local festival or your own anniversary, and to give returning customers a reason to look again.

How it works for your site

  1. Pick a moment. Choose the season, event or anniversary you'd like to mark.
  2. We design it to match you. Colours, wording and artwork are fitted to your brand, not copied from ours.
  3. You see it first. We check it on a private preview, on phones and computers, before it goes live.
  4. It switches off again. Themes are easy to remove, so your normal design is back when the moment passes.
