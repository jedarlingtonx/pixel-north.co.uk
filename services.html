Web design services for small businesses

I design and build websites for small businesses, in Northwich, across Cheshire and anywhere in the UK. Here's what I can do, in plain English. Tell me what you need, and I'll tailor a package that fits.

Website design and build

A custom, mobile-friendly website that looks sharp and says clearly what you do, designed and built from first sketch to launch.

Good for: new businesses, businesses with no website yet, and anyone whose current site is past saving.

Website refresh

A tired or confusing site, tidied up. I freshen the design and simplify the structure so customers can find what they need quickly.

Good for: a site that mostly works but looks dated or is hard to use. I'll tell you honestly whether a refresh or a new build suits you better.

Content and copy support

Help shaping your pages, from the structure to plain-English wording, so your site is clear and easy to read.

Good for: owners who know their business inside out but find it hard to put it into words.

Search and analytics setup

The basics that help people find you: search foundations on your site, help with your Google Business Profile, and simple analytics so you can see how the site is doing. Analytics is set up with a cookie choice for your visitors.

Good for: businesses that rely on local customers finding them online.

Enquiry forms and bookings

Contact forms and enquiry or booking tools that send customers straight to you.

Good for: businesses that want a simple, reliable way for customers to get in touch or book.

Care and support

Hosting, backups, updates and small edits, so your site stays safe and up to date after launch.

Good for: anyone who wants a site that just keeps working, without having to look after it themselves.

How quotes work

  1. An initial consultation. We talk about your business, your goals and what you need from a website.
  2. A clear quotation. I send you a written quotation covering what's included, the timescale and the cost.
  3. Start when you're ready. No pressure. If it looks right, we get going. If not, you've lost nothing.

I don't publish a price list, because every business needs something different. You'll know exactly what you're getting, and what it costs, before anything starts.
